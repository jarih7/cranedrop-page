Privacy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy describes how information is handled when you use CraneDrop, a pixel-art city-building game. CraneDrop is developed independently and includes advertising provided by Google AdMob.

Information collected by CraneDrop

The game itself does not collect, store, or send personal information to the developer. We do not operate an account system or use the information you enter in the game to identify you.

Advertising and Google AdMob

CraneDrop displays ads through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive information from your device when ads are requested, shown, or interacted with. Depending on your device, settings, location, consent choices, and the ad configuration, this may include:

  • Advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • Ad views, clicks, and interactions, as well as app and device information; and
  • Diagnostics and information used to detect fraud, limit ad frequency, measure ad performance, and provide or personalize ads.

This information is handled by Google and its partners under their own privacy terms. The developer does not receive or control all information that AdMob may collect or how Google uses or retains it. Ad personalization and data use may vary according to your choices and applicable law.

For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can manage advertising and privacy choices through the controls available in your device settings and any consent choices presented in the game. On Apple devices, you can review app tracking permissions in Settings. Device-level controls may limit certain uses of advertising identifiers, but may not stop all ads or all data processing by the ad provider.

Children’s privacy

CraneDrop does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad delivery and any related data handling may depend on the app’s audience settings, your location, and applicable requirements. If you have a concern about information handled in connection with the game, contact us using the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the game or its advertising arrangements change. The latest version will be posted on this page, with its revision date above.
